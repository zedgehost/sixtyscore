2. Description of the App

60Score is an offline-first cricket utility software designed to assist scorers, tournament directors, clubs, and sports fans in:

  • Recording ball-by-ball cricket match scores, extras, boundaries, and dismissals.
  • Calculating rain-interrupted target scores according to the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method.
  • Organizing and scheduling tournament brackets, groups, playoffs, and automated Net Run Rate (NRR) standings.
  • Conducting interactive player auctions and squad drafts.
