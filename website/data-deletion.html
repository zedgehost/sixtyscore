Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play Policy & GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure) Compliance

Google Play Compliance Notice: 60Score respects your privacy and provides full transparency. You have the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of your account and any associated cloud data.

1. Overview of Data Storage

60Score is an offline-first application:

  • Local Data: Match scorecards, drafts, and tournaments created without an account are stored solely on your device. You can immediately wipe all local data by going to Android Settings > Apps > 60Score > Storage > Clear Data or uninstalling the app.
  • Cloud Account Data: If you created an account via Google Sign-In, Email, or Phone to sync tournaments across devices, your account records are stored securely on Firebase. You can submit a deletion request below to purge this data permanently.

2. What Data Is Deleted Upon Request?

When you request data deletion, we permanently purge:

  • Your authentication profile (User ID, Email address, Display name, and Phone number).
  • All cloud-synced tournaments and custom squad rosters associated with your account.
  • All server logs and diagnostic traces containing your device identifiers.

3. Data Retention Period

Upon receiving your verified request, all personal data is deleted within 7 business days. No backup archives are retained beyond this processing period.

4. Submit a Data Deletion Request

Please fill out the form below or email us directly at privacy@60score.com using the email address associated with your 60Score account.
