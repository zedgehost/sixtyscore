Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: 60Score operates on an offline-first design. You can score matches, manage tournaments, and conduct player drafts without creating an account or transmitting personal data. If you choose to sign in for optional cloud backups, only minimal necessary account data is securely stored.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 60Score ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (Package ID: com.cricscore.cricket_scorer_app) and related online services.

By downloading, installing, or using 60Score, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Data Storage

60Score is built with an offline-first foundation. All match scores, balls bowled, commentary events, tournament brackets, and player lists are stored locally on your device using secure local storage (SharedPreferences / Local Device Cache).

  • You do not need an internet connection to score matches.
  • Your local match data remains exclusively on your device unless you explicitly opt into online synchronization.

3. Information We Collect (Optional Cloud Features)

If you choose to create an account or sign in to synchronize your tournaments across devices, we collect only the minimum required information via Google Firebase Authentication:

  • Email Sign-In: Your email address and encrypted authentication credentials. We never see or store your raw password.
  • Google Sign-In: Your display name, email address, and profile photo URL provided by Google's OAuth services.
  • Phone Verification: Your mobile phone number solely for sending a one-time verification code (OTP).

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track your precise GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements or use ad-tracking SDKs.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide authentication and infrastructure, 60Score interacts with trusted industry-standard service providers:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, in-app updates, and platform compliance. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): Used for secure cloud sign-in and encrypted tournament syncing. Firebase Privacy Policy

6. Data Security & Encryption

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • All network transmissions between the App and Firebase servers are strictly encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Local storage is isolated to the App's protected sandbox directory on the Android operating system.
  • Authentication tokens are handled securely using industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocols.

7. Children's Privacy

60Score is intended for general audiences interested in sports scoring and is suitable for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete such data from our servers.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

You have complete control over your data:

  • Right of Access & Correction: You can review and edit your local scorecards, player names, and tournament profiles at any time inside the App.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and any associated cloud data.

To submit a formal data deletion request, please visit our dedicated User Data Deletion Request Page or email us directly at support@60score.com.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
