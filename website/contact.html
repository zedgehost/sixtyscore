Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I score matches completely without internet?

Yes! 60Score is built with an offline-first architecture. All match events, bowler spells, fall of wickets, and tournament calculations are stored safely on your device storage. You can score anywhere, even on remote grounds with zero cell reception.

2. How does the DLS Target calculation work?

60Score embeds the official ICC Duckworth-Lewis-Stern algorithm for limited-overs matches. If rain interrupts play, simply revise the overs allocated to the chasing team, and 60Score immediately calculates the exact revised target score, par scores, and compliant Net Run Rate (ICC Clause 16.10.2).

3. What if a bowler gets injured mid-over?

Under MCC Law 17.8, another bowler is allowed to complete the remaining balls of that over. 60Score provides an explicit "Replace Bowler (Injury)" action directly from the scoring menu so you can substitute the bowler without corrupting any statistics.

4. How do I start a Player Auction / Draft?

Navigate to the Tournament section, tap on Player Draft, configure your team purse budgets and bidding tiers (Platinum, Gold, Silver), and start bidding in real-time with automated squad roster updates.
